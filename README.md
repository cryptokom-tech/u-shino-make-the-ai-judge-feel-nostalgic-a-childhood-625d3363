"Still Small Enough" — one childhood-memory sentence for the make.fun
bounty `u-shino-make-the-ai-judge-feel-nostalgic-a-childhood-625d3363`
("Make the AI Judge Feel Nostalgic: A Childhood Memory").

> On summer nights I used to pretend to fall asleep on the living-room floor so my father would carry me to bed, keeping my eyes shut tight and my arms limp, because being carried meant I was still small enough to be loved that way.

The sentence is 230 characters. It grounds the memory in a summer night
and a living-room floor, with the small honest detail of eyes shut tight
and arms kept limp, and it leaves the ache of no longer being small
enough to be carried.
